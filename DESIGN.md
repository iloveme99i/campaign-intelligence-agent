# Interface contract — Campaign Intelligence

The full release contract is maintained in
[`docs/product/05-interface-direction.md`](docs/product/05-interface-direction.md),
including THESIS, OWN-WORLD, STORY, FIRST VIEWPORT, FORM and QUALITY BAR.

The product is an evidence-led campaign analysis agent for chain retail and local-services merchant operators. It should feel restrained, operational, and data-dense: decision task first, activity scope second, deterministic outcome third, structured decision record fourth, trace details last. Agent is a capability, never the primary navigation object.

- Manrope is the working typeface; Space Grotesk is limited to the compact product mark.
- Cobalt is reserved for selection, focus, and primary action. Red/green always accompany a sign or label.
- Surfaces are separated mainly by rules and tone, not stacks of floating cards.
- No slogan, decorative hero, fabricated business outcome, or technical process above the business result.
- Empty free-form prompts never carry the task definition. Guided decision intent and risk controls precede optional business context.
- Desktop uses a persistent record rail; mobile uses a compact record selector. Tables scroll horizontally below 768px.
